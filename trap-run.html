Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Trap Run (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a free browser game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Trap Run, how it is used, and the choices you have. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous player ID. The game generates a random player ID and stores it in your browser’s local storage. It is not linked to your name, email address or any account.
  • Google sign-in (optional). You can sign in with Google using Firebase Authentication. Signing in is never required to play. If you sign in, Google provides your name and profile photo so the game can show them in the menu and use your Google name on your Profile and the Leaderboard. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase under Google’s privacy policy. You can sign out at any time from the menu and keep playing as a guest.
  • Gameplay analytics. To understand how the game is used, our server receives gameplay statistics such as your country, the levels you play, session length and similar usage data, tied only to the anonymous player ID.
  • Payment information. If you buy coins, you pay with USDT on the BEP20 (BNB Smart Chain) network and give us the transaction ID (TXID) of your payment so we can verify it. We never see, ask for or store your wallet password, seed phrase or private keys.
  • Advertising data. Our advertising partners, Adsterra and Monetag, may collect information such as your device type, browser, IP address and cookie identifiers in order to serve and measure ads. This data is collected and handled by the partners under their own privacy policies: Monetag Privacy Policy, Adsterra Privacy Policy and Adsterra Cookies Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the game and save your progress, coins, settings and unlocks on your device.
  • To analyse gameplay in aggregate and improve the game.
  • To verify coin payments and credit purchased coins.
  • To keep the game secure and prevent cheating and abuse.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We and our advertising partners use cookies and browser local storage. Local storage holds your game save and your cookie consent choice; advertising cookies are used by Adsterra and Monetag for ad delivery, frequency capping and measurement. See our Cookie Policy for details and opt-out links.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with the advertising partners named above, and payment TXIDs are checked against the public blockchain, where transactions are publicly visible by design. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Children’s Privacy

Trap Run is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes described above. Data stored in your browser’s local storage stays on your device until you clear it, which also erases your game save.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and the CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data associated with you, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, use the in-game Contact form. For a deletion request, send a message from the Contact form with subject “Privacy — Delete my data” and include your Game ID, which is shown in your Profile. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available from the game menu, with the date of the last update shown at the top.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy: please use the in-game Contact form (menu footer → Contact) and include your Game ID, shown in your Profile.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Cookies are small files stored on your device by your browser. Trap Run also uses browser local storage, which works in a similar way. This policy explains what is stored and how you can control it.

1. Essential Storage

  • Game save. Your progress, coins, settings, unlocks and anonymous player ID are stored in local storage so the game can continue where you left off.
  • Consent choice. We store your cookie consent choice so the cookie notice is not shown to you again.

This essential storage is required for the game to work as expected. Deleting it erases your saved progress.

2. Advertising Cookies

Our advertising partners, Adsterra and Monetag, may set and read cookies on your browser. These are used for ad delivery, frequency capping (limiting how often you see the same ad), ad measurement and fraud prevention. They are governed by the partners’ own policies: Monetag Privacy Policy, Adsterra Privacy Policy and Adsterra Cookies Policy.

3. Managing and Deleting Cookies

Most browsers let you view, block and delete cookies and site data in their settings (usually under “Privacy and security”). Blocking all cookies may stop ads from loading correctly, and clearing site data will delete your Trap Run save and show the cookie notice again.

4. Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising

  • Industry opt-out: aboutads.info/choices
  • Monetag: see the opt-out information in the Monetag Privacy Policy.
  • Adsterra: see the Adsterra Cookies Policy.

5. Changes and Contact

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time; the current version is always available from the game menu. Questions: please use the in-game Contact form (menu footer → Contact).
